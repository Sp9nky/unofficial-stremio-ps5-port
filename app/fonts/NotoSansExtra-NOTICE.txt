(Regular/Bold for Hebrew and Thai, Medium for CJK) and cut down to the characters of the common
legacy character sets (JIS X 0208, GB 2312, Big5, KS X 1001 / all Hangul syllables) without layout
tables. They are modified versions and keep the Reserved Font Name rules of the license.
